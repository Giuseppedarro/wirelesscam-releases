# Privacy Policy for WirelessCam

**Last Updated: April 2026**

WirelessCam ("the Application") is proprietary software designed to stream video and audio from an Android device to a PC. Your privacy is our priority, and this document explains our data handling practices.

## 1. Camera and Microphone Access
The Application requires explicit permission to access your device's **Camera** and **Microphone**.
* **Purpose:** These permissions are used solely to capture real-time video and audio for streaming to the connected WirelessCam PC client.
* **Local Streaming:** All data is transmitted directly over your local network (Wi-Fi) from the Android device to your PC.

## 2. Data Collection and Storage
* **No Server/Cloud Uploads:** WirelessCam does NOT record, store, or upload your video or audio data to any external servers or cloud services.
* **No Personal Information:** We do not collect personal data such as names, email addresses, or location information.
* **Ephemeral Processing:** All video and audio processing occurs in real-time. No data is saved on the mobile device after the streaming session ends.

## 3. Third-Party Sharing
We **do not share**, sell, or trade any data captured by the Application with third-party companies, advertisers, or services.

## 4. Security
As the Application operates within your local network, transmission security depends on your local network configuration. We recommend using a secure, password-protected Wi-Fi connection.

## 5. Contact
For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may open an issue on the official GitHub distribution repository.
